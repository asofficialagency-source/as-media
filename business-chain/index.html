The model

Three links. Every business.

01

Attention

Content, ads, outreach, referrals. Anything that makes a stranger aware you exist.

Broken looks like quiet months you cannot explain.

02

Acquisition

Everything between noticing you and paying you. The next step, the capture, the follow up.

Broken looks like views that never become conversations.

03

Fulfillment

Solving the problem you promised, and having the capacity to do it well.

Broken looks like clients who do not come back.

The rule

One weak link and the answer is zero.

You cannot be good at two out of three. Acquisition matters, but a perfect middle link with nothing coming in at the top is just as useless. If one link does not get your full attention, it breaks the other two with it.

Three strong links multiply. Two strong links and one weak one still comes out at zero.

Where it goes wrong

Attention is visible. Fulfillment is your craft.
Acquisition is invisible, so nobody builds it.
