Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

AS Media ("we") runs an internal tool to schedule and publish our own social media content. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

What we collect

When we connect our own accounts (for example TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), the platform gives us an access token and basic profile info (name, avatar, account ID). We use this only to publish and schedule our own videos and to read their performance stats.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or rent data. We do not collect data from other users. Tokens are stored locally and securely and are never shared with third parties.

Deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings. To ask us to delete data, email as.official.agency@gmail.com.
