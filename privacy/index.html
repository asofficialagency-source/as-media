Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

AS Media ("we") runs an internal tool to schedule and publish our own social media content. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

What we collect

When we connect our own accounts (for example TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), the platform gives us an access token and basic profile info (name, avatar, account ID). We use this only to publish and schedule our own videos and to read their performance stats.

Google user data (app "AS Media Posting")

The tool connects only to AS Media's own Google account. With our permission it uses: YouTube (upload and manage our own videos, read our own analytics), Gmail read-only (read the form notifications our own website forms send us), Google Sheets (read and update our own CRM and finance sheets) and Google Drive read-only (list our own files). This data is processed on our own computer, only to run our own business. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

AS Media Posting's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or rent data. We do not collect data from other users. Tokens are stored locally and securely and are never shared with third parties.

Deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings. To ask us to delete data, email as.official.agency@gmail.com.
