Frequently asked questions

How long until we see results?
The foundation goes live in days, not months. Qualified conversations follow once the trust and conversion layers are running, and we tune the system every week. We won't put a fake number on it, because trust takes real time to build.
Do you make the content, or just advise?
Both. Strategy and the execution, scripts, editing, production, are handled inside the system. You stay the voice; we handle the machinery around it.
Do you guarantee results?
No, and we won't pretend to. What we guarantee is the system: how it's built, run, and improved. Results depend on your offer, your close, and your market, and we're honest about that.
Do I need a big audience already?
No. The system is built to attract the right people, not to depend on your follower count. If you already get attention, the gap is conversion, not reach.
What if I only run ads right now?
That's fine. Plenty of founders come to us getting attention one way and no system behind it. We build the organic and conversion layer around what you already do.
